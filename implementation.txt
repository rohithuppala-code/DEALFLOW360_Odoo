STEP 16 — DISCOUNT GOVERNANCE ENGINE

Create a FastAPI service dedicated to discount evaluation.

For each quotation line determine:

requested_discount

effective_allowed_discount
excess_discount

A stricter applicable ceiling should control the line.

Conceptually:

effective_allowed_discount =
minimum(applicable tier limit, applicable category limit)

line_excess =
max(0, requested_discount - effective_allowed_discount)

Do not calculate this only in React.

React displays results returned by FastAPI.
